# Project Notes

Location: TUMCA Biodiversity Credit project proposal
**Paula Sanchez** - Managing Director, CAMPO Capital is a company that manages environmental projects. 40 years working in this sector. Also offer consulting services. The goal is to bring capital to the countryside. Most projects in Colombia.

Carbon sequestration projects with Verra, restoration and forest management projects. 6mil USD under management from investors. Clients as investors. Several development banks.

They have projects: Manacao Cocoa project in colombia, Ortensia project, Campo herbs

https://www.campocapital.com/en/managed-projects/

They work with "Green Economy Partnership". They are sponsoring the project. GEP signed an agreement with Government of Zanzibar. This company will have exclusivity to develop carbon and biodiversity projects. GEP has now hired CAMPO to implement the project. https://greeneconomy.ae/

When generated, where would the credit go?

- the standard we are going to use is CERCARBONO, is a standard already approved ACCRO standard (like other standards PLANVIVO, VERRA, etc...). We are expecting a very similar approval process for this. The benefit-sharing agreements will be similar.

What is the timeline for accreditation?

- According to this timeline, we are going look for an investor to look at all the process to issue biodiversity credits. At least 1 or 2 years down the line.
- We will propose a socialization process. They have a model already developed. Funding will go to,
  - MPA protection
  - Livelihoods
  - Community support (other than livelihoods)
  - Administration
- Gov has not asked for a percentage yet. GEP is managing this dialogue.
- It also has a PIN and PDD document. Similar process. Innovative stage. The work along the project developers to develop the methodology. CAMPO CAPITAL takes care of this.
