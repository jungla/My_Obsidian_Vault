## 1. Nairobi Convention COP12 Planning (Oct 5–9, 2026)

The group discussed leveraging the upcoming Nairobi Convention (NC) COP12 to advance the OECM agenda.

- **Proposed Schedule & Format:**

  - **Oct 3–4:** Working Group internal meeting to progress the group's programme of work.

  - **Oct 5 (Special Sessions Day):** Organize a dedicated session/panel discussion on OECMs targeting WIO-C partners and government representatives (NC Focal Points, National Directors, etc.).

- **Session Focus:** Discussing the advisory role of the working group for governments and presenting key OECM Policy Brief findings. Shauna Mahajan suggested this should definitely feature a panel format.

## 2. Upcoming Products & Deliverables

The group discussed potential upcoming products and "shared products" to support under existing or future funding:

- **Policy Brief Webinar:**

  - Organize a 2-hour public webinar to present the current report and discuss its recommendations. Maya Pfaff was proposed to present.

  - Shauna noted this could potentially link with an ongoing Nairobi Convention webinar series; Eduardo clarified this webinar would be for the general public.

- **Community Engagement & FPIC Guidance:**

  - Develop guidance on how communities should be engaged and how Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC) can be embedded along the OECM process without adding undue work/burden, ensuring communities directly benefit. (Proposed leads: Vatosoa, Maafaka, and others).

- **Monitoring & Shared Tools:**

  - Harmonizing monitoring via shared tools, aiming for a 2027 _"Stewards of the Coastline (OECMs)"_ report. (Proposed leads: Shauna and others).

- **Project Integration:**

  - Embedding OECM "best practices" into existing NGO projects. (Proposed leads: Jean and others).

- **Documentary / Media:**

  - Jean suggested creating a documentary explaining what OECMs mean for the WIO region. Tanguy noted the need to think through the format and audience. Eduardo shared an existing UNEP introductory video on OECMs as a reference point.
