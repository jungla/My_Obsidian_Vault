**Background & Strategic Context**

- **Policy Brief Baseline:** A policy brief summarizing status, assessments, and suggestions for Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures (OECMs) in Tanzania and the broader Western Indian Ocean (WIO) region was recently released.
- **Upcoming Nairobi Convention COP:** Scheduled for October (1 month out), presenting a major opportunity to present OECM implementation plans to government focal points and political representatives.
- **Target Area Focus:** Focus is primarily on community-managed and co-managed areas, such as Collaborative Fisheries Management Areas (CFMAs).

**National Framework & Governance Status**

- **Lead Government Body:** Vice President’s Office (VPO) acts as the national lead for setting up the overarching OECM framework and reporting to the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD). Martha Golowera serves as the active VPO focal person.
- **Line Ministries' Role:** Line ministries (e.g., Ministry of Livestock and Fisheries - MLF, Ministry of Natural Resources and Tourism - MNRT) will sit on the National Technical Working Group and handle ground-level management/governance.
- **National Roadmap Delays:** A national roadmap was drafted following an August meeting in Morogoro, but progress stalled while waiting to finalize Terms of Reference (ToRs) for a consultant funded by GIZ. GIZ has tentatively identified candidates, and the consultancy is expected to launch in 1–2 months to facilitate a 6–9 month national review process.
- **NGO Consortium:** An IUCN-led NGO consortium (8–10 member organizations) is collaborating on national OECM efforts. Key contact for IUCN: Philomon Mene.

**Work Plan & Next Steps**

- **Parallel Field Assessments:** NGOs do not need to wait for the national legal framework; field assessments and site readiness can proceed using standard IUCN guidelines.

- **Action Item (OECM Work Plan Document):**
  - All participating organizations are tasked with populating the shared work plan (covering governance, capacity building, livelihoods, etc.) on SharePoint.
  - **Target Deadline:** Complete inputs in 10 days (by September 21st).

- **Government Engagement:** Share the finalized work plan with VPO and CBD focal points before the COP to ensure alignment, secure buy-in, and avoid hostility or disownership.

- **Position Paper Proposal:** Consider drafting a CSO position paper outlining existing community-managed sites (CFMAs) to urge formalization and policy support.

**Internal Alignment Needs**

- **Communication Gap:** Improved communication is required within larger NGOs (e.g., WCS, WWF), as terrestrial and marine representatives are not always aligned on parallel task force discussions.
- **Marine & Terrestrial Integration:** While the overarching national framework will integrate both terrestrial and marine realms, marine-focused entities will coordinate inputs through their specific working group to feed into the broader national task force.
