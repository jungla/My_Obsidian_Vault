Intro meeting between WCS Tanzania and Blue Ventures to map overlapping work in Pemba East and PECCA and identify collaboration opportunities. Both sides will share activity details and community lists to avoid duplication, with a WCS stakeholder meeting in December as a next touchpoint.

### Meeting Purpose

- Blue Ventures convened the call to learn about WCS Tanzania's work and explore collaboration, especially in overlapping seascapes.
- Acknowledged parallel global-level conversations underway between WCS and Blue Ventures.

### WCS Tanzania Marine Program Overview

- Marine program run from Zanzibar with field offices in Tanga and Pemba; country office in Dar es Salaam.
- Around 10 years of work across the Pemba Channel, supporting ~50 communities on MPAs, livelihoods, and policy.
- Strong science role: ecological monitoring, national-scale surveys, data shared with government on reefs, mangroves, fisheries.
- - Supporting two new MPAs in Pemba East and planning a new project for a proposed MPA in Kilwa.
- Other work: blue carbon/mangroves in Tanga, coral reef restoration, shark conservation, VSLAs, MPA capacity and infrastructure.

### Blue Ventures Work in Tanzania

- Works through partners, not as direct implementer; 20 partners across the coast, 4 in the Pemba-Tanga seascape.
- Supports 53 communities in Pemba-Tanga: 26 under Mombau (Mkinga, Muheza, Tanga City, Pangani) and 27 in Pemba via Jumakiko, Piro, Peseo.
- Four pillars: community-based fisheries management, Secure Rights (governance), financial inclusion (VSLAs), and food security.
- Pemba onboarding done this year; year one focuses on foundation-laying and baseline data (household surveys, fisher profiling).

### PECCA Overlap and Coordination

- Blue Ventures initially excluded PECCA to avoid duplication with WCS, IUCN, GIZ, but UNDP interest reopened the area.
- About 53 BV partner communities fall within PECCA via Mombau, Peseo, and Piro; government pointed BV to WCS and IUCN.
- WCS supported creation of co-management plans for all Pemba East SFCs and CMAs; now focused on livelihoods and MCS implementation.
- - Many community-planned activities remain unfunded and could be entry points for BV support.

### Other Collaboration Threads

- WCS data available via a Zenodo group and the Data Mermaid coral reef repository; access on request.
- Ministry rolling out an online fishery licensing system (WCS-developed under USAID); fishers must complete training to register.
- - WCS flagged this as a potential fit for BV's rules-and-regulations sensitization work.
- WCS partnership model: complementarity (e.g. Milele on beekeeping/seaweed) or geographic split (e.g. Mombau on governance in Pemba East North, Msuka Bay, 10 vil
- Action for the Ocean will be WCS's local partner entry point in the new Kilwa seascape.
