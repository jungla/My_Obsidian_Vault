Victoria walked Aaron - WCS Ruaha Katavi Landscape Tanzania and Jean Mensa through the WCS Impact Platform data sets and asked Tanzania to prioritize partners and scholarships. Species and library validation are deprioritized; quarterly reminders plus a yearly validation with attached lists were agreed as the preferred cadence.

### Context and Scope

- Africa is lagging Latin America and Asia on completing the global Impact Platform data sets.
- Metrics are high-level (partners, species, publications, scholarships) and not tied to day-to-day field work.
- Aaron - WCS Ruaha Katavi Landscape Tanzania and Jean Mensa are the current Tanzania points of contact across the data sets.

### Partners Data Set

- Editors use the Data Explorer to review the country partner list, edit entries, add missing ones, and flag duplicates.
- Only current partners are in scope; lapsed ones get an end date rather than deletion, historical partners are not back-filled.
- Stronghold linkage has caused cross-country contamination in the past and should be watched.
- - Cross-country strongholds (e.g. Gulf of Guinea) can wrongly attach partners to neighbouring countries.
- CSV export is not usable; editing must happen in the web UI, which is not user-friendly.

### Scholarships Data Set

- Scope now covers WCS Global Scholarship Program plus thesis and research support for hosted or externally-supervised students.
- Kate Mastro records global scholarship program entries; country teams add locally-supported students.
- Tanzania has roughly a decade of historical scholarship data that could be entered.

### Species, Library and Process Feedback

- Species list (from IUCN overlay) is long, unsorted and impractical to validate without taxa expertise; group pushed back on the exercise.
- Speaker 3 will raise smarter alternatives with the global team rather than force country validation.
- - Suggestion: disclaimer that list is IUCN-derived, and long-term consult external taxa experts.
- Library validation via Madeline Thompson's automated system works acceptably; Tanzania will keep validating suggested additions.
- Preferred cadence: quarterly reminders plus a yearly validation email with the partner list attached to reduce friction.
