Malvika Bhansali interviewed Jean Mensa to gather input for OBF secretariat recommendations. WCS pilots livelihood projects seeking private-sector scaling, and sees OBF's role as both connecting investors and exerting leverage for sustainable practices. Potential collaboration flagged on an upcoming EU eco-tourism project.

### Purpose of the Call

- Pangea consultancy is nearing completion; input from NGO members will shape recommendations to the OBF secretariat
- Goal: capture WCS priorities in the blue economy and how OBF can better support members
- Emerging themes from other NGOs: positioning NGOs as de-risking partners and strengthening execution partnerships

### WCS Blue Economy Approaches

- WCS pilots small-scale, innovative livelihood interventions and seeks private partners to scale them into sustainable businesses
- Produces scientific work (e.g. noise propagation studies affecting marine mammals) intended to inform port authorities and shipping
- In Madagascar, WCS is more hands-on, supporting company creation and sitting on boards (e.g. chocolate export from livelihood projects)
- Challenge: WCS wants to exit pilots, but connections to private investors are ad hoc rather than systematic

### Role for OBF

- Act as a marketplace where private companies can discover NGO-piloted projects as business opportunities
- - NGOs would present portfolios of pilots for investor scoping
- Exert leverage on private sector to adopt sustainable practices based on NGO scientific findings, not just broker connections
- Support designing smart projects that bring private sector in early (Malvika Bhansali's example: bank concessional financing for hotel energy transitions)

### Blue Tourism Collaboration

- WCS has an upcoming EU-funded eco-tourism project in Zanzibar and Kilwa seascapes, community-led and pilot-scale
- Also aims to professionalize existing operations (dolphin tours, snorkeling, Sea Turtle Aquarium) to improve tourist experience
- Precedent set in Tanga region partnering with local tour operators to sustain community businesses
- Malvika Bhansali flagged Climate Gateway Africa as a potential partner for blended donor + bank concessional financing models
