Esther and Phil, researching a wildlife series, spoke with Jean Mensa about filming potential on Latham Island, its bird colony and marine life, logistics, and other story ideas around Zanzibar and Pemba.

### Latham Island Overview

- WCS ran the first full ecological survey of Latham two years ago, two three-week missions, backed by a USAID project.
- Island sits in coastal waters but is far enough offshore to be nearly untouched, with very light fishing pressure.
- Rich pelagic fauna: sharks, dogtooth tuna, spectacular bird colony, and likely an important marine mammal migratory hotspot.
- Water visibility around 20-30 meters; presumed unique enough to justify a future MPA designation.

### Survey Setup and Logistics

- Used a catamaran liveaboard anchored offshore plus smaller boats; bird team camped on the island with GPS and drone.
- - About 8-9 scientists on the liveaboard, ~10 more on the beach, teams rotated weekly.
- Cheaper alternative: two small fiberglass boats from Zanzibar with local pilots, roughly \$500 fuel for a short mission.
- Big boat takes ~20 hours; supply boats 1-2 hours depending on sea state, so resupply runs are feasible.
- No phone reception on the island; satphone or Starlink needed for comms.

### Filming Considerations

- Best season is November, with November-January workable; February gets weather-dependent.
- Camping on the beach is sheltered by a low cliff of compacted guano; main downside is the smell.
- Occasional fishing visitors from Kizinkazi, mostly weekends; spearfishers leave the beach empty during the day.
- Research permit was obtained through Zanzibar; both governments were invited but Zanzibar is the closer partner.

### Wildlife Story Potential

- Full food-chain scenes observed: mako shark hunting tuna hunting sardines, with birds feeding above.
- Coral reef bleached in 2024 but bounced back unusually fast; large groupers and fish rare elsewhere in Zanzibar.
- Spinner dolphins seen regularly on the surrounding shallow plateau; humpbacks expected in season but absent the year they surveyed.
- Chick predation not observed, but team was there before chicks fledged; Matteo would know later-season behavior.

### Other Regional Stories and Projects

- Pemba pitched as culturally compelling: traditional Swahili fishing communities, less touristed than Unguja.
- Whale sharks present in Pemba but hard to find; Mafia Island is the reliable spot. No known manta aggregations.
- WCS planning shark tagging at Latham (hammerheads) and Maziwe (reef sharks) next year; humpback whale tagging off Tanga in season.
- WCS has no sea turtle program; NGO Under the Wave does tagging and some (controversial) egg translocation.
