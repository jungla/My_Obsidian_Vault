> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> Blue Action Fund grantees in Africa brainstormed a joint presence at Impact 6 in Senegal for the fund's 10-year anniversary. A shared stand, side event, and pre-conference knowledge-sharing workshop emerged as leading ideas.

### Purpose and Grantee Coordination

- Meeting set up to coordinate a joint Blue Action grantee proposal for Impact 6 in Senegal; abstracts due mid-November.
- Meirinhos drafted a list of African grantees, partly from AI using the Blue Action website, so it may contain errors.
- Large group with sub-grantees is hard to manage; spreadsheet to be used to mark who wants to take part.
- Meirinhos initiated the idea; Cecilia (Blue Action Fund) also emailed asking about a joint Impact presence.

### Impact Participation Options

- Options: collective side event (90 min, about €1,000), 90-second video, poster or visual map, and a stand (about €2,000).
- Artist or community-led contribution seen as too costly for grantees far from Senegal.
- Blue Action Fund should be involved from the start if the effort celebrates its anniversary.
  - Raised in chat; Meirinhos agreed, especially for the stand
- Joint video judged hard to coordinate; no in-house videomakers identified.

### Madagascar Side Event and Common Themes

- Silvia Poggioli shared that Blue Ventures already agreed with Marcus to co-host a Blue Action side event on Madagascar's national EBA framework.
  - Other grantees could get a separate joint activity in addition
- Tanguy Nicolas noted EBA mainly fits Western Indian Ocean grantees; common ground across Africa is MPAs and coastal livelihoods.
- Meirinhos proposed themes: MPA sustainability after creation, community inclusion in decisions, and continuity after funding ends.

### Shared Stand Concept

- Stand pricing on website appeared to be 222 per square meter (likely euros), so cost depends on size.
- Ideas: enlarged OOC project photos, a 10-year timeline of achievements, and an Africa map of projects and funding volumes.
- Staffing needs a rota; regional theme days (Western Indian Ocean, West Africa, Southern Africa) and one-to-one meeting slots suggested.

### Pre-Conference Workshop and Costs

- Taylor proposed a two-day grantee workshop before Impact producing a joint output like a policy brief or statement.
  - Seen as more useful than pre-scripted one-hour panels
  - A rapporteur or consultant could capture findings
- Meirinhos supports it, noting value depends on how many of the about 10 projects attend.
- Registration is 350 (non-OECD) or €500 (OECD) per person, covering meals and shuttles; side event €1,000 if paid before 31 January.
