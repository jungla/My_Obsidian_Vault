> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> Ramadhan presented a master's research concept comparing coral gardening and direct transplantation for reef restoration in the Tanga Marine Reserve system; the group endorsed it and suggested refinements to title and objectives.

### Research Concept Presentation

- Concept 1 (coral gardening vs direct transplantation in Tanga Marine Reserve system) was most supported in email feedback
- Objectives cover growth, survival and mortality, bleaching, performance by coral genera, and environmental influence
- Research questions will be restated as hypotheses in the proposal

### Feedback on Title and Objectives

- Iswalala suggested shortening the title to the core comparison; study site details go in the proposal
- Iswalala noted the last problem-statement bullet reads more like significance of the study
- Johnson Mshana suggested merging objectives one and two, and three and four, keeping the environmental objective separate
- Environmental conditions should be listed specifically (e.g. temperature, turbidity, dissolved oxygen)

### Supervisor and Timeline

- Supervisor is allocated after University of Dar es Salaam registration, likely from outside SOAF
- Iswalala warned delays could stretch the master's to four or five years
  - Agreeing objectives with a supervisor early allows data collection to start safely

### Clarifications on Objectives

- Ramadhan asked whether combining growth, survival and mortality, or bleaching and genera performance, is scientifically sound
- Johnson Mshana: bleaching is feasible even with six months of data; master's results need not be conclusive
- Bleaching affects restoration performance, so objectives three and four can be combined; run correlations with environment

### Next Steps

- (Ramadhan) Register at the university next Monday to get a supervisor allocated
- (Ramadhan) Reach out to IMS experts to share the research idea and gather input
- (Ramadhan) Revise title and objectives per feedback in the proposal

### Decisions Made

- Concept 1, comparing coral gardening and direct transplantation, is the chosen research idea
