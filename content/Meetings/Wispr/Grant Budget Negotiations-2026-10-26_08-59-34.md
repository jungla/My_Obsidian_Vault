> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> The team reworked the \$900,000 Tanzania grant budget after the funder said salaries, operating costs and ICR near 50% would not pass review. They agreed to cut Kenya, trim staff lines, add match funding and boost core activities.

### Funder Pushback and Context

- Funder lead <@person3:n=Barney%20Long> said salaries, operating costs and 10% ICR near 50% of budget "will not pass review"
  - Also flagged 18 WCS salaries with zero co-financing and expects some salaries inside the 10% indirect
- Bennett sees a flawed system: small marine grants are expected to fund large institutional running costs
- Funder caps NICRA at 10%; internally WCS normally targets about 30% bulk admin per project

### Reconciling the Numbers

- Funder-visible donor template shows about 55% non-activity, versus 50-50 in the internal budget
- Gap is data collectors (about 45K, 5% of total) mapped under 'other' as the funder advised, though they are activity costs
- Removing Kenya (about 48,500 to 49K) to activities brings non-activity to about 50%, effectively 45% excluding data collectors
- Bennett refused reclassifying staff as activities, calling it cooking the books; better to be open on cost categories

### Activity Adjustments

- 3.1 NDF workshops (about 22,000): <@person3:n=Barney> thinks SCF already funds it, but SCF global grant does not cover national work
- 2.2 species management plan kept as a deliverable; funder called it low value, team sees it as key for government focus
- Jean Mensa wants Kenya savings to go to 4.2 MCS and 5.3 eDNA, the core and costliest components
- 2.1 spatial fishery restrictions (about \$29,000 to 30,000) is the only part the funder valued
  - Match from non-SCF sources (e.g. Ocean Accelerator Bloomberg MPA work in Kilwa) would look better to <@person3:n=Barney>

### Next Steps

- (Marwa) Make the budget changes and run a final check with Kiecha before sharing back with the team
- (Bennett) Email the list of required changes and what can be allocated as match funding
- (Bennett) Highlight which activity lines to increase and where match funding goes under activities
- (Kiecha) Remove the Kenya allocation from the budget and redirect it to activities

### Decisions Made

- Kenya removed entirely; its salary and office costs move to Tanzania activities
- Drop about four very small salary lines (around \$1,000 each) without reallocating the money to other salaries
- Global comms position stays, since the funder explicitly asked where comms is budgeted
- Add salary and activity match funding from IWT and SCF, roughly mirroring per-line amounts
- Keep 3.1 and 2.2 deliverables; cut 3.1 budget and move its \$4,100 consultancy to the IUCN Red List group
- Increase 4.2 MCS and 5.3 eDNA; raise 2.1 by about 15% with significant match funding
