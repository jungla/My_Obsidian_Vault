> Budget review of the revision that removed Kenya office costs and shifted funds to activities, reaching a 60-40 activities-to-staff ratio; Marwa will document salary and office cost changes for the donor submission.

### Kenya Cost Removal and Reallocation

- Kenya office lines (personnel and office costs) were removed from the draft budget; no other regional cuts at that stage.
- Goal was to move from a 50-50 split to 60-40 activities versus admin; Kenya savings alone were not enough.
- Communication supplies moved into activities and some activity costs raised; activities rose by about 82, from 447 to 530.
  - This left budget 30k above, cut from other admin and staff costs
- Jean Mensa initially expected only Kenya savings to be reallocated, so Bennett was called in to clarify.

### Staff and Office Cost Cuts

- The 30k cut was split across country office, Zanzibar and regional office in the existing proportions.
- Bennett saw country office salaries unchanged in the comparison sheet; Marwa had not updated it after the last rework.
  - Country office cost actually went from 52 to 44
  - Combined staff and office for field teams went from 123 to 108
- Program staff, now including support from <@person3:n=Martin%20Kita%20Mwasembe>, is at 94.

### Donor Perspective

- Budget now reaches a 60-40 ratio of activities versus staff cost, expected to satisfy the donor.
- Donor had said the 50-50 budget would never work; the 10% ICR goes to New York and does not cover salaries.
- Bennett frames salaries, office costs and ICR together as an institutional cost package that was reduced.

### Next Steps

- (Marwa) Update the comparison sheet to reflect the revised workings
- (Marwa) Email a list of all salary and office cost changes, replying to this morning's email to Jean Mensa and Bennett
- (Bennett) Add the salary and office changes to the draft narrations and share with <@person3:n=Till> for approval before submitting

- Budget adopts a 60-40 activities versus staff cost ratio, with the 30k shortfall cut proportionally across offices
