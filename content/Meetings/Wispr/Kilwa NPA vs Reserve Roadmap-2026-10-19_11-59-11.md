> Jean Mensa dictated an NPRU (likely MPRU) update for Simon Lugando covering the Kilwa protected area roadmap, the Tanga marine park management plan, and ecotourism support.

### Kilwa Protected Area Project

- Main joint work with NPRU is the Kilwa project ("UNPA", likely an MPA); discussed at the Nairobi Convention meeting sidelines
  - Attendees: manager Sueike, Warden Ndipula, WCS, AFO, two from Tahuiri (name unclear), who manage the Kilwa ruins
- Key open choice: convert existing CFMAs into an NPA, or establish marine reserves within the CFMAs
  - NPA option raises questions on community roles, financing, rights, and BMUs transitioning to VLCs
- Reserve option mirrors Tanga, where reserve islands sit inside CFMAs: easier, but a smaller role for NPRU
- A plan to extend NPRU's mandate to all marine resources, absorbing fisheries roles, could make the two options converge

### Kilwa Roadmap and Process

- A roadmap is being developed: pick NPA vs reserve internally, then present and refine the plan with stakeholders
- After a field visit and ground consultations, a large regional partner meeting will present the way forward

### Tanga Marine Park Management Plan

- GMP for Tanga "Civica" Marine Park (likely Coelacanth) slowed while UCN (likely IUCN) contracts a consultant
- GIZ support sought for stakeholder consultation; plan is a UCN, WCS, and GIZ partnership
- Main obstacle is the baseline assessment: collecting NPRU's requested data from WCS and partners before consultation starts
- WCS proposes making community CMA plans an annex to or part of the GMP, rather than separate documents

### Ecotourism Development

- A consultant is on the ground assessing ecotourism opportunities in the Tanga marine park
- Funding is available to build ecotourism infrastructure to support the park's financial sustainability

### Next Steps

- (WCS team) Conduct a field visit in Kilwa to kickstart ground teams and the consultation process
