> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> The Pemba site visit is set for two days (Sunday and Monday): day one with WCS in Msuka, including afternoon snorkeling at the coral nursery, and day two with Mambao/FFI.

### Visit Dates and Constraints

- Visitors reach Zanzibar Saturday by ferry, attending a Norway-Tanzania climate partnership stocktaking in Dodoma the week before.
- Embassy proposed a ministry meeting Monday or Wednesday morning; Taylor noted WCS's main partner is the Ministry of Blue Economy and Fisheries.
- Projects hold community meetings Tuesday and a stakeholder meeting Wednesday, plus a Mambao steering committee on 2nd December.
- Pemba is a 20 to 40-minute flight from Zanzibar, so the embassy could join for a one-day trip.

### Day One: WCS in Msuka

- Msuka showcase village: ranger station, community coral restoration, credit and saving groups, community-led patrols, possible tree planting.
- Nearby Kanoa shows nature-based solutions such as repaired dikes stopping saltwater intrusion, inside the newly gazetted marine conservation area.
- Morning meetings and presentations, then snorkeling at the coral nursery (500 to 800 meters out) after lunch at low tide.
  - Visibility uncertain given 3-meter (Sunday) and 2.8-meter (Monday) tidal drops; water shoes needed
- Partners like Oikos or Malele could join, but extra attendees add allowance costs.

### Day Two and Visitor Interests

- Day two in west central Pemba, led by Mambao/FFI: mangrove and coral restoration, eco credits, MSMEs, women's economic empowerment.
- Nesbakken wants a short presentation and informal talk with implementing partners' results management and monitoring staff.
- Hole wants to see contribution to the partnership's climate adaptation goals; Nesbakken also wants to see how partners interact.

### Logistics and Etiquette

- WCS will help book accommodation (basic or high-end options); visitors pay on arrival and should carry cash.
- Taxis from pre-approved vendors, booked a couple of days ahead, since project vehicles may be busy.
- Passport details go to government in advance due to spot checks; visitors travel on diplomatic passports.
- Pemba is conservative: cover hair with a light scarf, prefer long sleeves; book flights via Skyscanner, arrive 45 minutes to one hour early.
