> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> Stakeholder workshop in Tanga for an EU-funded nature-based solutions (NBS) scoping and feasibility study; participants agreed an NBS definition and began mapping NBS activities by location and partner.

### Project Overview

- Study falls under the EU Sustainable Western Indian Ocean Program (heard as 'SWIA'), pillar three, output two: upscaling NBS
  - Commissioned by likely Expertise France (heard as 'Executive France') to 'SQL Ecosolutions' (name possibly garbled)
- Study maps existing NBS, assesses scale-up potential, reviews blue carbon and innovative finance, gathers community priorities
- Feeds a call for proposals expected in December, about €5 million total, one project per seascape
  - Seascapes: Tanga-Pemba-Mombasa, Primeiras e Segundas (Mozambique), and one heard as 'Sayer Lumara'
- Five revenue streams assessed together: blue carbon, biodiversity, fisheries and marine resources, climate resilience, ecotourism

### Defining Nature-Based Solutions

- Swahili working term proposed: 'suluhu za njia za asili'; sessions mix English and Swahili
- WCS participant noted NBS and EBA are often used interchangeably; they treat NBS as interventions only
  - Their NBS: coastal erosion work, earthen dike repair, tree planting for urban cooling
- Facilitator presented the IUCN definition; participants noted it omits climate change

### Community Examples (Mwambao)

- Mwambao community restored about 1000 mangrove trees in degraded areas; bees kept to deter mangrove cutting
- Artificial reefs built where past dynamite fishing damaged corals
- Three CFMAs in Tanga and Mkinga district focus on marine conservation and erosion prevention
- Seaweed farmers trained to make products: seaweed flour, oil, soap, lotion, plus honey, raising income

### Group Exercise: Mapping NBS Activities

- Each table lists NBS, specific location, partners involved, and challenges addressed; 30 minutes, then 5 to 7 minute reports before 11:00 tea
- Location should be as specific as possible; Pemba included, scale (e.g. hectares) wanted
- WCS activities listed: mangrove restoration (10 sites), coral restoration (3 sites), LMAs (about 9), seagrass restoration, temporary and permanent closures
- Same activity by different partners to be listed under one heading with multiple locations; fuller details via Excel

### Decisions Made

- NBS definition uses 'socioecological challenges' instead of 'societal challenges'
- Study covers both narrow NBS interventions and wider measures like policy, bylaws, and livelihoods
