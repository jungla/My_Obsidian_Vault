> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> OBF NGO members validated five feedback themes and discussed the OBF2.0 value proposition; private sector linkages emerged as the most valued service. Pangea will refine the proposal for the secretariat.

### Member Feedback Themes

- Malvika Bhansali framed the shift from network building in 2025 to bankable projects in 2026.
  - Pangea has engaged 30-plus donors; some fund blue economy with little knowledge of it
- Five themes from one-on-one member talks:
  - Clearer value proposition; less process, more action; marketplace linking ideas and capital
  - Scaling and replicating over pilots; stronger consortia and smarter project design

### Resource Mobilization and Peer Engagement

- Joan Momanyi said CODIL's innovation clusters produce good concepts, but funding is the gap, e.g. the Fisheries Innovation Cluster.
- Sheila Ruto cited online fatigue and wants the secretariat to run peer-to-peer engagements in a calendar of events.
- Speaker 6 noted time and budget limits make big events (about 70% of members attend) the practical meeting point.
  - Suggested reviving innovation teams; tourism and blue financing teams worked well

### Private Sector Engagement

- Jean Mensa said OBF's heavy process obscures its focus and turns private sector members into NGOs.
  - Hopes the GIZ and European Union incubator will clarify OBF's value proposition
- Sheila Ruto's CSI pilot in Kenya shows NGOs underestimate the patient capital and trust-building private partners require.
- Jonas Tesfu said no method yet exists to align incentives with nonprofits; it is still a sandbox.
- Speaker 6 described a gap between science and private sector; intermediaries like Ocean Innovation Africa and SoteHub help.

### Pangea Progress and OBF2.0

- Most advanced concept is green port shipping (lead sounded like 'Pamesa'): four ports, total investment of 250 million.
  - Main challenge is mobilizing money fast enough to keep partners motivated
- Donor work: submitted to Schmidt Marine; engaging Oceans 5, Climate Works, Lloyd's Register Foundation, Dawn Foundation.
- Proposed NGO value proposition: funding model transition support, project design standards, market-linked approaches, joint proposals, credible impact platform.
- Jean Mensa found it vague; WCS most values linking community initiatives to private investors who can take them over, not grant support.
