> First official OECM working group members' meeting: countries updated national work plans (final by end of year), the template timeline was revised, regional priorities reviewed, and the work plan vote moved online.

### National Work Plan Updates

- Mozambique: national dialogue set for first week of December, will discuss a roadmap; no major changes expected to the plan.
- Tanzania: no work yet; a November meeting with the national working group will clean up the plan by year end.
- Kenya: workshop on the 10th to harmonize two assessment tools, then national workshop 17th to 20th to finalize tool and OECM framework.
  - ICZM working group meeting planned for November to bring the OECM agenda into formal government forums
- Madagascar: legal framework in place; plan covers regional working group branches, site assessments in two seascapes, awareness raising.
  - Funded by JEF 6 MPA (likely GEF 6, ends this year), then a GBF fund also developing financing mechanisms

### Work Plan Template and Indicators

- Jean A. Mensa proposed adding success indicators or milestones; Deidre De Vos suggested tracking progress against the policy brief roadmap.
- Options: clarify the policy brief alignment column or add a separate indicators section, while keeping the template simple.
- Jean A. Mensa suggested grouping activities under higher-level objectives, since some plans read like a laundry list.

- Plan participation in the SADC (heard as 'Sadak') regional OECM meeting in Kenya, 2027; <@person3:n=Eduardo> listed as contact.

- Ecological and governance monitoring workshop for a cross-WIO platform, likely 2027; WCS has some Tanzania funding.
  - Also: annual report, collation of country OECM readiness assessments, tracking YOC presentations at events
