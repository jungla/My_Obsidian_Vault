> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> Planning an IMPAC side event on OECMs: the side event format is favoured, aiming for a cross-regional African dialogue (plan A) with a WIO-only fallback. Submission deadline moved to the 30th.

### Submission Logistics

- IMPAC website now shows the submission deadline as the 30th, not the 16th
- Submission types: oral scientific presentation, poster, sharing experience, artistic activity, video communication, side event
- Side event fee is about 1,000 euro, per EduardoVideira, found under registration/participation info

### Choosing the Format

- Durations unclear; an AI search by Jean A. Mensa suggested side events run 60 to 90 minutes as parallel sessions
  - Sharing experiences: 15 to 20 min per speaker or 60 to 90 min workshop
- Sharing experiences form asks for case-study content: introduction, solutions, lessons learned, replicability, transferability
  - Jean Mensa felt this does not fit the group's case
- Side event form needs only a title and concept note (likely 500 words); EduardoVideira and Jean Mensa lean toward side event

### Content and Framing

- Possible content: OECM communication toolkit (if progress allows), work plans presented at Nairobi Convention COP, OECM concept intro
- Shauna Mahajan (she/her) - WWF suggested agreeing a high-level message, e.g. OECMs as a mechanism to strengthen community rights and governance
- Idea of cross-regional exchange with West Africa and Benguela, e.g. Ghana's inshore exclusion zone as a potential OECM
- Check whether IUCN's global OECM programme is planning something similar, via their contact <@person3:n=Daniel>

### Proposed Session Structure

- About 20 minutes of presentations (work plan, possibly WIO report from BCC if ready), then roundtable with presenters
- Mixed panel: government, community, CSO/NGO and possibly donor representatives from each region
- IMPAC seen as more practitioner-focused than UNOC or OOC, allowing more informal, unscripted discussion

### Decisions Made

- Pursue plan A: cross-regional OECM dialogue; revert to WIO-only if no outside interest within a week or two
