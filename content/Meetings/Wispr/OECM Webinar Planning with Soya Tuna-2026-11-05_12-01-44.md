> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> First planning call for a joint webinar on OSCMs (likely OECMs) for CSOs with Swayatuna. Set for 10 December, 90 minutes, as the first of a webinar series.

### Context and Goals

- Swayatuna (likely the SWIO tuna forum) is a CSO network in 7 countries with over 200 members.
  - Idea to also bring in WAIO and MMA Alliance (likely WIO-LMMA Alliance) to widen reach
- Swayatuna wants an introductory session: what OSCMs are, how they relate to members' work, and opportunities.
  - Most members see OSCMs as a gray area
- YSC working group sees it as outreach for its regional OSCM roadmap, with a mailing list of about 100 interested people.
- Eduardo Videira wants a group discussion to collect CSO feedback for a planned product on socializing the OSCM process.

### Webinar Format

- Date moved from end of November to 10 December (a Thursday) because Swayatuna has its AGM then.
- Agenda: about 30 min presentations, 15 min Q\&A on basics, about 45 min breakout group discussion.
  - Two intro slides each from Swayatuna and the LMMA Alliance, then OSCM basics
  - Possibly play a 'what is an OSCM' video
- Breakout groups of about 10, each with Swayatuna/CSO and working group facilitators and a recording sheet.
- Run a survey via Slido (QR code) to profile the audience and their OSCM knowledge.
