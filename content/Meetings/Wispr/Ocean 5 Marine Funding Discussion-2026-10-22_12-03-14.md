> Informal marine team call on making Nairobi Convention engagement more structured and on the Ocean 5 proposal rewrite, amid concerns about senior-level funding and site decisions.

### Angola and Nairobi Convention

- Simon is in Angola launching a terrestrial program, a park near the Zambia border; marine work there depends on oil money.
- Collins already submitted short outcome statements to the Nairobi Convention after the COP.
- Bennett wants a structured, two-way approach: a convention contact who explains deadlines, steps and feedback.
  - Years of white papers and recommendations got little traction
  - Member governments (e.g. Tanzania) carry more weight than NGOs
- Collins wants an agreed internal structure on who engages whom; <@person3:c=e3b5d568-97a3-44b3-b37c-498087567f1f> seen as well placed, close to Arthur.

### Ocean 5 Proposal Rewrite

- Proposal now focuses on two components: establishing new MPAs and effective management of new and existing MPAs.
  - Northwest is the main focus, some Antandre Bay; Atomovata dropped (likely Ambodivahibe)
- Dierkens needs urgent activity lists with budgets from Red, Dave and Tim, e.g. receiver cleaning, coral surveys.
- Lead and final budget still unclear: WCS alone at around 1 million versus a consortium.
- Atomovata funding goes to BV as manager; Theo may also want to drop Antanjil Bay, which the team wants to keep.
  - Dierkens can show coral, seagrass and other results to prove the site matters

### Funding Strategy Concerns

- Collins sees terrestrial and senior managers deciding marine site priorities without biologists' input.
- Bennett expects less unrestricted marine funding and pressure to pursue only large grants like Blue Action Fund 5 million euro.
  - Current \$900,000 project has 450,000 in salaries and office costs; funder and Theo disagree
- Dierkens suggests small grants could still cover national or site-level work.
- Madagascar cannot currently approve small grants without regional sign-off; Bennett and Dierkens think it should.

### Internal Grant Selection (IWT)

- A new IWT round was likely already allocated internally and the deadline has probably passed.
- Program development picks which country concepts advance; Bennett and Wato find it opaque and unfavorable to marine.

### Unrelated Park Tourism Discussion

- A follow-up meeting is planned, possibly first week of November, to decide before replying to consultants.
