> Jean Mensa reviewed existing projects in the Pemba and Tanga seascape (World Bank, EU, Blue Ventures) to identify coordination and complementarity with the proposed 'block shrimp farm' project (name likely garbled).

### Pemba: World Bank project

- Government-implemented 'Tasman' World Bank project (name possibly garbled) invests multiple tens of millions of dollars in Pemba.
  - Work plan covers governance, capacity building and MPA facilities
- Known plans: a ranger station in south of the MPA (Pemba East), staff hiring, data collection capacity.
- WCS aims to coordinate with government and stay complementary in Pemba.

### European Union projects

- Bahari Yetu regional project implemented by TNC, IUCN and WCS; WCS covers coastal forest, mainly Unguja.
  - TNC ecological mapping is slow; outputs pending, planned ecosystem atlas
- \$5 million EU grant to WWF and 'Mombau' (likely Mwambao) covers areas including Pemba; contract being signed.
- OICOS and WCS Marine Programme ecotourism project funds community-led to private sector initiatives, targeting Pemba East.
  - Could complement community-led ecotourism in the proposed project
- SWIOP pillar via Expertise France will subgrant; WCS hopes for a Kenya-Tanzania transboundary NBS project.
  - Currently under scoping; potential expansion to Pemba seascape and Nkinga District (likely Mkinga)

### Blue Ventures community grants

- Blue Ventures launching grants of a few tens of thousands of dollars each for CBOs across Pemba East villages.
- Targets CBOs rather than 'cheyotes' (likely shehias), but complements WCS co-management work; WCS is in touch.
