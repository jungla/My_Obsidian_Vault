### Spatial Units and Project Scope

- Target species has three broad genetic areas: Malaysia eastward, Northern Indian Ocean (India, Sri Lanka), and Kenya southward.
- Year 1 work with geneticists will refine East African population structure, to see if fishing elsewhere (Somalia, Sri Lanka) affects the same population.
- The East African unit is split into Tanzania and Zanzibar because Zanzibar has separate fisheries regulations.
- Out-of-scope units were listed only on earlier guidance (from <@person3:n=Kelsey>). The recovery objectives column read as a commitment, causing the confusion.

### Co-targeted Species (Jitensis)

- Historically recorded in Tanzania and caught extensively in Mozambique, but never recorded in Tanzanian or Kenyan catches; possibly fished out.
- Option 1: keep it, adding fishing mortality and recovery indicators, optionally noting the work is contingent on finding the species.
- Option 2: remove it from the recovery pathway. It then leaves the project: no measurement and no dedicated budget, only a loose mention in the proposal form.
- Confirming presence by year 1 is unlikely, so a kept indicator might show nothing for the whole project.
- Jean Mensa noted monitoring new landing sites (Somanga, Kilauea, as heard) might reveal it, so a monitored number could still be useful.
- If kept, all related monitoring and threat actions must appear in the activities column with matching timelines.

### Indicator Formatting

- Non-detriment finding (NDF) indicator counts one government declaration barring exports, not trade volumes: baseline 0, target 1 from year 1.
- Baselines and targets must be numeric values only (counts, percentages). Units go in the indicator text, notes, or means of verification.
- Example: "fishing mortality reduced by 80%" can add "(measured in landings per survey day)" in the indicator text.
- Recovery indicators must be separate per species; rows 26 and 27 already are.
- Row 22 should say increase, not decrease.
- Training indicator: baseline 30 trained, target 60 by year 1.
- Cumulative vs per-year targets (patrols, protections) are unresolved pending a check of prior project rulings.
