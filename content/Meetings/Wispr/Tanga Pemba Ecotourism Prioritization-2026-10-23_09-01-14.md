> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> Team reviewed the consultants' Tanga and Pemba ecotourism prioritization deliverable. Villages were accepted, but the report must clarify MPRU fees and licensing and extend costing beyond the top five.

### Deliverable Overview

- Consultants scored sites through four gates into bands (high priority, viable with conditions, reserve, not recommended) plus implementation confidence
- Only Mgini Ole flying fox heritage day (Pemba) is high priority, high score and high confidence, likely because the product already exists
- Tanga's TASAMP / MPRU marine park island day sits just below the top five

### MPRU Licensing and Fees in Tanga

- Jean Mensa: all Tanga options except the MPRU-run island day need MPRU registration and licensing, a likely deal breaker
- Jean Mensa worried a full MPA in Kilwa (vs a reserve) would similarly kill small tourism enterprises
- Report lists a flat \$20 park fee, but that applies only to non-East Africans; real fee structure is far more complex
  - Entrance: 10,000 shillings East Africans, $20 non-Africans, $10 residents; tour guide fee \$50 per year
  - Public services/restaurants in parks: $25,000, $10,000 or \$3,000 by category
- Jean Mensa wants clarity on whether each initiative serves MPA revenue or community livelihoods; Taylor noted it falls under livelihoods for BAF

### Costing Scope

- Next deliverable is costing, contractually for the top five only
- Jean Mensa insists costing covers all 10 Tanga options, since final selection depends on financials plus feasibility
- Taylor believes consultants already weighed fees but did not show it; D3/D4 could be reordered, SWOT could include entry barriers

### Site and Village Review

- Pharles Ramadhan praised the Tanga product number three as innovative and possibly region-wide; Sahare walk should link to nearby TASAMP island
- Taylor questioned team capacity to implement some products, e.g. Kiyombeezi dance and Tanga coastal school packages
- Jean Mensa stressed community buy-in as the main success factor
- Pharles Ramadhan flagged incorrect terms (e.g. 'village resource committee') and wants a Word version for field review

### Next Steps

- (Taylor) Ask consultants to state MPRU licensing barriers and real park and business license fees explicitly in the report
- (Taylor) Request costing for all options, not only the top five, and add TASAMP island day to the Tanga list
- (Taylor) Request the Word version of the report and share it with the team for comments
- (Taylor) Email consultants to set up a quick call and send them the fee regulations document
- (Johnson Mshana) Report back on how MPRU manages community ecotourism groups on Mafia Island after speaking with the warden
- (Johnson Mshana) Review the official MPRU fee structure and look for room to negotiate within the regulations

### Decisions Made

- Proposed villages accepted for Tanga and Pemba; Pemba's four visited villages approved, Pujini needs rechecking
- Report can proceed, pending the financial costing and fee clarifications
