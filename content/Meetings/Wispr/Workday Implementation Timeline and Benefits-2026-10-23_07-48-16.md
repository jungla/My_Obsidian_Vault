> [!summary]- Flow Summary
> Speaker 1 presented the Workday HR system rollout timeline (go-live expected April next year) and its benefits; Kasuga began demoing employee and manager self-service in the WCS Work Hub.

### Implementation Timeline

- Planning ran May to September; ambassador network chosen by colleague votes in August to September.
- September to December is system testing; December to April is first deployment before launch.
- Workday expected to go live around April next year, with training starting alongside launch.
- Training and updates shared by email to leaders and ambassadors in phases due to regional time differences.
  - Training to teams expected February and March (year stated as 2017)

### Benefits of Workday

- Two sections in one channel: employee self-service (ESS) and manager self-service.
- Managers gain team visibility and can complete common manager tasks online.
- Timesheets let colleagues allocate time to grants, projects, and cost centers, easing leave and project tracking.
- Employees can update personal info, request time off, view balances, access learning, pay, benefits, and online performance reviews.

### Employee Self-Service Demo

- After logging into WCS Work Hub, available features depend on whether the user is a manager or employee.
- Dashboard has Person, Organization, and Home; personal info, names, contacts, and emergency details are editable.
- Organization chart shows manager job details and contact information.
- Absence feature replaces paper leave forms sent to HR: pick dates, then submit the request.

### Manager Self-Service

- Managers access team information in one centralized location; demo was cut off at this point.
