> The proposal keeps the species, adding a fishing mortality indicator held at 0 and cumulative targets. The Tanzania eDNA collaboration with the MESCAL team is moving ahead, but bottom-dwelling species need deeper sampling.

### Proposal reviewer feedback

- The reviewer's comments were confusing and often answered a different question; requests like splitting by species were already done.
- Decreased mortality can't be an indicator since fishing mortality is already 0; removing the species would mean removing all related activities and content.
- The new indicator is somewhat fictitious: it will likely stay at 0, and any increase couldn't be measured.
- Reviewers asked to replace 'not applicable' with '0', which created contradictions, e.g. catch per unit effort from 0.06 to a year 2 target of 0.
- Cumulative targets avoid this: species protected 0 to 2 then stays 2, NDFs 0 to 1, patrols 12, 24, 36, 48.

### Tanzania eDNA collaboration (MESCAL team)

- <@person3:n=Theo> and the advisor <@person3:n=Toma> are both keen to collaborate on samples; next is matching each side's targets and sites to avoid duplicated effort.
- Argali (eDNA company, contact <@person3:n=Eva>) includes 2 primers in the price, so fish and elasmobranchs (heard as 'iguanasma branks') can run at all sites.
- Running only one primer barely lowers cost, so the earlier idea of splitting sequencing by taxon is unnecessary.
- Argali quoted 60 euros for extraction, which seems high; Montpellier might extract cheaper, but eDNA expertise matters more.
- Design: 3 replicates per site, each sequenced with 3 PCR replicates for 90 euros, giving 9 sequences per site.

### Sampling design and water column depth

- <@person3:n=Theo> had no clear survey design and leaned toward random sampling; Jean Mensa prefers systematic sampling in likely habitat so the samples are usable.
- The towed 'yellow submarine' filters about 100 liters in half an hour but only at the surface; eDNA is stratified, so bottom species like wedgefish may be missed.
- Ideas: weight the submarine to tow deeper, below the surface layer, and sample near shore where shallow, wave-mixed water reaches close to the substrate.
- A large battery pump is the alternative but is a logistical burden.
