## Mangrove group

They have nursery and do patrols. They get paid for performance and are building a school with the revenues. They had a meeting with the community and they had agreed to do the school. They also have some revenues that are for them. The mangrove was destroyed by taking worms. They are working to reduce the practice, which is not illegal.

They have heard of the project for the MPA and know a little what the APGC is.

Government asked why don't they farm something else? They answered that what they get from the ocean they get from the mangroves.

In TZ the revenues go in the VSL groups. Could be interesting to do that here also.

They want to have the funds that are used for the reward as a fixed cost of the MPA. A way to manage the community funds.

The funds could be also coming from blue carbon but also offsetiprograms. There is a private company nearby that needs to offset.

They have areas reserved for swimming .

The market, is not at the landing site. It's for traders only. Not much fish with quite a bit of reef fish. Apparently fishers all have agreements with buyers and sell all to them directly because the traders own the gear and provides the fuel. There is no auction. We will visit the landing site one of these days.

## Day 4

### No take zone

9ha of seagrass restoration zone. Some technique. New project. Anchoring and people walking and fishing was a problem. Also beach seines. 4 communities. 2 where they only do rehabilitation and 2 where they do active restoration. They want to see if there is a difference. They do the same in mangrove areas. This a permanent closure. They want to move the closure every two years to see if this increases production. Similar to what people do on for agriculture.

The community is very good and completely isolated. There is no road.

The seagrass is very sparse. The restored patches are there, and it's probably not fished but it needs better demarcation with a sign. It will take time to recover the area is bare sand ATM with a very few patches of seagrass with algal growth on top.

### OECM

The government and NGOs agreed on a process for recognition of OECMs.

1. The government is preparing a set of guidelines to clarify the process and criteria for approval of proposals
2. Proposals are submitted by proponents to the CBD focal point, under the department of environment
3. The focal point will then convene a panel of experts to review the proposals according to the guidelines
4. If accepted, the CBD submits the proposal to the CBD

The WCS plan did not include any support for ANAC and the MPA (except for the baseline surveys). It's kind of unfair to say that we supported the process for 10 years. We only did the work with communities.
