What are OECMs: CBD definition. Areas with clear governance structure and demonstrated effectiveness. Recognized by Governments and registered in the international OECM database

**Agenda**

- Introductions - about 12 ppl participated, all from WIO-C member institutions (WCS, WWF, IUCN, CORDIO)

- Jean presented a brief summary of last WIO-C meeting to introduce why we came together

- Past work from this group
  - it's the first time WIOC has a working group on OECMs
  - but there is an informal regional group, the CBRMN, that has done a lot of work on OECMs and LMMAs. The WIOC OECMS WG should decide if it makes sense to incorporate/replace that group and on what to focus to avoid overlap.
  - Notable outputs from that group include (please share anything available on these initiatives or others),
    - LMMA forum in Mada,
    - report on the status of OECMs by CORDIO,
    - IUCN has done work through BNA support,
    - WIOMSA also has done mapping of LMMAs in KE, MZ, MD and TZ,
    - Capacity building tools discussed and developed,
    - WWF has published a report in effective and equitable Governance of OECMs/LMMAs

- Participation
  - Check with NC/WIOMSA to see if ppl not in the WIO-C can join the working group.
  - We propose a structure with a,
    - Core group with representatives from WIO-C members only for most meetings
    - Task committees with broader participation to work on specific problems ([[Policy Briefs|Policy Briefs]], protocols, etc...)

- Objectives
  - We kept the discussion as a first brainstorming opportunity. What we will focus will be decided in person at the meeting pre-WIOMSA and will depend on what has already been done in this space and what the needs are. We want to avoid replicating work and overlapping focus with CBRMN
  - Proposed objectives include
    - Study on optimal locations for OECMS
    - Study on policy framework and enabling conditions for OECM assessment and recognition
    - Study on benefits & incentives for OECM recognition (what's the benefit for communities in being in recognized OECMs?)
    - Development of systems and protocols to monitoring governance and management effectiveness
    - Development of standard protocols to monitor ecosystems, socio-economics and governance in OECMS
    - Establish baseline information (what exists that we can leverage on) and define a guiding framework
    - develop a 'regional policy brief' with a summary of where we are now (what's being done) and where we need to go

- Plans for next WIOMSA Symposium
  - The priority at this stage is to define objectives for the WG that are aligned with what is needed and do not overal with past work in this space. The goal of the meeting pre-WIOMSA is to define these objectives
  - In preparation for that meeting, we will collate available information in that space and share it with the group
  - We will also make sure to invite to the pre-meeting key representatives from IUCN and CORDIO that can report back on the activities of the CBRMN group
