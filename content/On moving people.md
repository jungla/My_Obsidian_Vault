I don't really know how to move people. This happens at all levels. They either request too much and every request feels effectively the same, which is ineffective, or they don't request enough, and they drop the rock in the pond, and they assume that things will move from there.

The reality is that you have to be quite strategic and only push things, and this, I think, is one of the main limiting factors in good [[On leadership]]. You need to know how to push. I have examples of insuccesses in both cases: people who just continuously request (this makes it very ineffective) or people who don't request enough.

This is also, I think, quite effective in banning people, by the way, especially the requesting too much problem. It's important to give clear indications and to make sure people can understand when an indication is actually important versus noise. It has something to do with the noise versus signal issue. If everything is important, nothing really is.
