**Welcome & Context**: Invite everyone and clarify that joining this conversation is the beginning of a process. Mention that this work has been ongoing for nearly two years with the working group, and today's presentation represents a major milestone.

**Progress Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that while only about 10% to 20% of the long-term journey is complete, the group is fully active and moving forward.

**Working Group Objectives**: Discuss the core purpose of the working group and why this initiative extends beyond standard OECM (Other Effective Area-based Conservation Measures) efforts. We want to do more than 30x30. We want to do work that is relevant for communities, going beyond 30x30 targets, while still significantly contributing to them.

**Role of OECMs & Communities**: Note that OECMs are not the sole solution and undue pressure should not be placed on communities. The goal is to recognize the positive contributions communities are already making toward conservation goals.

**Event Structure & Agenda**:

- **Phase 1**: Summarize past progress and outline the working group's vision and motivation after two years of defining its purpose.
- **Phase 2**: Present future plans, ambitions, and realistic goals. Highlight the group's strong local presence, solid relationships with stakeholders and governments, and effective multi-country coordination framework.

**We are developing a framework**, a system, to work across multiple countries, which is going to be the tool we use moving forward. This has worked very well in developing the policy brief first, and now in the work plan.

**Voluntary Commitment & Dedication**: Reiterate that the group's work is driven entirely by shared commitment and civil society dedication, operating without formal dedicated funding or specific project ties. This is a powerful approach that comes through in everyone in the working group.

**Closing & Next Steps**: Encourage an interactive, productive session where attendees gain a clearer understanding of the group's direction and find space to contribute.
