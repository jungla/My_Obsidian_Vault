**Fuel Efficient Stoves - EASY**
We have data collected on consumption of charcoal and wood for cooking pre- and post- distribution of fuel efficient stoves. Preliminary results show a decrease in the use of wood and increase in use of charcoal. Open [[questions]] remain on,

- transition from wood to charcoal reduces use of wood overall (it is more efficient to produce and burn charcoal rather than burning wood directly)
- what other benefits are there? Cooking time? collection time? health benefits? we have some data on this as well

**BNS scores and community wellbeing - MEDIUM**
We collected BNS score across tens of communities in Tanzania. In some cases over several years in areas where we have projects. Preliminary analysis show significant differences across regions and across groups. Open [[questions]] remain on,

- gender imbalance in wellbeing
- impact of our conservation projects on wellbeing
- differences in wellbeing associated to livelihoods and geographies. Fisherfolks are better off than the rest of the communities in rural areas but not in urban areas. This affects the effectiveness of alternative livelihood projects.

**Climate Vulnerability and wellbeing - MEDIUM**
We are working to refine a CVA we conducted based on social surveys on instances of climate related threats and their impacts and their association with community wellbeing. We want to develop and calculate a Climate Risk Score that include the BNS score for Vulnerability, the Probability of climate related events (how often they occur), and the damage they result in (Exposure). We have data on most of these variables through social surveys, but we need [[to do]] the statistical analysis.

**Minimizing [[Sharks and Rays]] landings - HARD**
We have a few years of data on [[Sharks and Rays]] landings at several locations in Tanzania. We collect information on time/date, gear used and area. From the data it looks like some combinations of gears, seasons and areas result in more/less landings of sharks. What we would like [[to do]] is to analyze the data to come up with a model that explains these relations and allows us to propose regulations on gear/season/area that minimize landings of sharks (some species at least).

**Value-Chain improvements analysis - EASY**
We have been collecting data on value chain improvements for small-pelagic and reef fisheries. Our interventions mostly focused on reduction of post-harvest loss but it seems that these resulted in measurable increase in revenues for several players in the value-chain. We would like to ensure we have good data and document these changes for different interventions and actors.

**MCS data spatial analysis - MEDIUM**
We are collecting data with GPSs on surveillance activities in the MPAs. Rangers go out with boats every few days and record their activities with an application called SMART. We would like to analyze the data to,
a) summarize type of infractions, actions, etc... (EASY)
b) develop a spatial distribution model that predicts distribution of infractions based on environmental and social inputs (geographies, type of fisheries in the area, type if management measures, level of Governance, etc...). This is HARDer.

**Fishery Patterns Mapping validation - HARD**
We conducted a large activity to map fishing grounds of local communities through community mapping and we were able to produce fishing pressure maps for the whole Zanzibar. We now have a partnership with a group that is trying [[to do]] the same but with GPSs. Their approach is costly and limited to certain areas but can be used to validate our approach. The goal of this projects would then be to find a way to compare the two datasets and quantitatively validate our the community mapping approach.
