# WCS-TNC MOU

## Purpose of the MoU

The purpose of this Memorandum of Understanding is to establish a collaborative framework that leverages TNC’s leadership in marine spatial planning, innovative conservation finance, and policy engagement alongside WCS’s expertise in marine science, hydrodynamic modeling, and community co-management to ensure coordinated action across shared priorities in the Western Indian Ocean.

## Geographical Scope of the MoU

The MoU's geographical scope covers the Western Indian Ocean countries where both TNC and WCS operate: Kenya, Tanzania, Mozambique, and Madagascar.

## Areas of Common Interest

- **Resilient Coral Reefs:** Identification of climate refugia, larval connectivity corridors, and scalable habitat restoration.
- **Marine Spatial Planning (MSP):** Multi-zone ocean planning incorporating ecological, social, and economic spatial data.
- **Innovative Finance:** Long-term ocean financing mechanisms, including debt conversions, blue bonds, and carbon frameworks.
- **Sustainable Fisheries:** Balanced stewardship across small-scale coastal fisheries and commercial/industrial management.
- **30x30, OECMs & MPAs:** Accelerated gazettement, boundary verification, and formal legal recognition of marine protected and conserved areas under global biodiversity targets.

## Institutional Roles & Strategic Division

### **WCS (Scientific Data & Field Co-Management Lead):**

- **Scientific Data & Research:** Conducts habitat mapping, fine-scale hydrodynamic/larval connectivity modeling, biological baselines, long-term monitoring, and socioeconomic catch assessments.
- **Site Execution & Co-Management:** Leads ground-level boundary verification, community co-management governance, localized reef restoration, and small-scale post-harvest value-chain support.

### **TNC (MSP Coordination, Conservation Finance & Policy Lead):**

- **Marine Spatial Planning Coordination:** Leads multi-sector process facilitation, spatial data integration, and technical support to embed conservation boundaries into official national ocean zoning.
- **Conservation Finance & Regional Policy:** Structures blue bonds, debt-for-nature mechanisms, and blended capital; leads high-level policy engagement under regional frameworks (e.g., Nairobi Convention, CBD).
- **Industrial Fisheries Engagement:** Drives offshore commercial fisheries transparency through Electronic Monitoring (EM), machine-learning catch tracking, gear/FAD bycatch reduction, and supply-chain sustainability initiatives.

## Strategic Operational Modalities

1. **Knowledge Exchange:** Sharing methods, tools, financial models, and lessons learned.
2. **Data Integration & Research:** Harmonize spatial, ecological, and socio-economic datasets under shared governance protocols to co-author scientific reports and support national and regional ocean reporting.
3. **Collaborative Fundraising & Implementation:** Co-designing proposals to match TNC's financial structuring with WCS's long-term field presence.
4. **Coordinated Advocacy & Policy:** Issuing joint position papers, aligning regional MSP guidelines, and advancing OECM recognition.
