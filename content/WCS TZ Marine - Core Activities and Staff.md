Key Activities

- Community work to support improved fishery management and conservation across 4 seascapes: Mkinga, TACMP, Pemba East and Misali,
  - Government capacity improvements
  - Management planning
  - Livelihood support: value-chain and value addition
- Support with capacity development for MPAs around the Pemba channel: TMRS, TACMP and Pemba East,
  - Demarcation
  - Infrastructure
  - [[Training Materials]] on coral reef monitoring
  - Training on SMART and MCS
  - Equipment and supplies for MCS
- Support creation of new MPA in Pemba East,
  - Community and government consultations
- Ecological monitoring,
  - Coral reefs
  - [[Sharks and Rays]]
